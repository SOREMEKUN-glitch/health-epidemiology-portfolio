# How Much Can We See? Estimated and Reported Tuberculosis in Five Countries, 2000–2024

This project compares WHO-estimated TB incidence, TB notifications, notification gaps,
and the age-sex distribution of estimated TB cases in Nigeria, South Africa, India,
Brazil and Canada.

## Data
- WHO TB burden estimates, downloaded 7 October 2026
- WHO estimated TB incidence by age group and sex, 2024
- World Bank Population, total (SP.POP.TOTL), 2024

## Methods
- Descriptive analysis of TB incidence and notification trends
- Calculation of estimated notification-gap rates and approximate gap counts
- Age and sex distribution of estimated incident TB cases in 2024

## Main findings
- South Africa and India showed substantial declines in WHO-estimated TB incidence.
- Brazil and Canada showed smaller overall changes after earlier declines and later increases.
- Nigeria’s unchanged WHO estimate and uncertainty interval cannot be interpreted as a trend.
- South Africa had the largest estimated notification gap per person.
- India had the largest approximate number of people represented by the notification gap.
- Estimated incident TB cases were higher among males than females in all five countries.
